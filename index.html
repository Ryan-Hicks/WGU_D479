Plan Your Trip

Getting here: Most visitors arrive by air; a weekly cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay.
Getting around: Rental cars, hotel shuttles, taxis, and public buses (5am–11pm) serve Taniti City. Bikes require helmets by law.
Good to know: USD is accepted island-wide; power outlets are 120V; the hospital has multilingual staff.
